How to pay from Cash App

  1. Tap Open Cash App above. The invoice is copied for you.
  2. In Cash App, open the Bitcoin section and tap Send.
  3. Paste the invoice (or scan the QR code below). Cash App fills in the amount.
  4. Check the amount and confirm the payment.
  5. Come back to this page. It shows Payment complete when your payment arrives.

Menu names can differ slightly between Cash App versions. Other Lightning wallets work too: paste the invoice or scan the QR code.
